# Behavioral Interview Prep

## STAR Method Framework

Structure every answer using STAR:

| Component | What | Time |
|-----------|------|------|
| **S**ituation | Context and background | 1-2 sentences |
| **T**ask | Your specific responsibility | 1 sentence |
| **A**ction | What YOU did (be specific) | 3-5 sentences |
| **R**esult | Measurable outcome | 1-2 sentences + learnings |

## Common Questions

### Conflict & Disagreement
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate/manager.
- Describe a situation where you had to push back on a decision.
- How do you handle conflicting priorities from stakeholders?

### Failure & Mistakes
- Tell me about a project that failed. What did you learn?
- Describe a time you made a mistake that impacted the team.
- When did you miss a deadline? How did you handle it?

### Leadership & Influence
- Tell me about a time you led without formal authority.
- Describe when you mentored someone or raised the bar.
- How have you driven technical decisions in your team?

### Ambiguity & Complexity
- Tell me about a time you had to make a decision with incomplete data.
- Describe a project where requirements kept changing.
- How do you prioritize when everything feels urgent?

### Delivery & Results
- Tell me about your most impactful project.
- Describe a time you delivered under tight constraints.
- When did you go above and beyond?

## Amazon Leadership Principles Key Tips

| LP | What they look for | Red flag |
|----|-------------------|----------|
| Customer Obsession | Start with customer, work backward | Only talking about tech |
| Ownership | Long-term thinking, no "that's not my job" | Blaming others |
| Bias for Action | Speed matters, calculated risks | Analysis paralysis |
| Dive Deep | Metrics, root cause, details | Staying too high-level |
| Earn Trust | Candid, self-critical, listen | Never admitting fault |
| Deliver Results | Focus on outcomes despite obstacles | Excuses without action |
| Have Backbone | Disagree and commit | Always agreeing |
| Invent and Simplify | Novel solutions, remove complexity | Over-engineering |

## General Tips

1. **Prepare 8-10 stories** that cover multiple LPs each
2. **Quantify results**: "reduced latency by 40%" > "made it faster"
3. **Use "I" not "we"** they want YOUR contribution
4. **Be honest about failures** show growth and self-awareness
5. **Keep answers to 2-3 minutes** be concise
6. **Practice out loud** it sounds different than in your head
7. **Ask clarifying questions** shows you think before acting
