# Project Management

## Core Skills

### Estimation
- **Techniques**: T-shirt sizing, story points, time-based, three-point (optimistic/likely/pessimistic)
- **Cone of Uncertainty**: Early estimates are inherently inaccurate new_textrefine as you learn
- **Tips**: Break down into small tasks, use historical data, account for unknowns
- **Anti-patterns**: Padding estimates, committing to estimates as deadlines

### Planning
- **Roadmap**: High-level direction (quarters), not a commitment
- **Sprint planning**: Detailed commitment for next 1-2 weeks
- **Dependency mapping**: Identify blockers early, parallelize where possible
- **Risk management**: Identify risks, assess probability × impact, plan mitigations

### Retrospectives
- **Format**: What went well? What didn't? What to try next?
- **Goal**: Continuous improvement, not blame
- **Action items**: Specific, assigned, time-bound
- **Frequency**: End of each sprint or milestone

### Stakeholder Management
- **Communication**: Right level of detail for the audience
- **Expectations**: Set early, update proactively when things change
- **Trade-offs**: Scope, time, quality new_textpick two
- **Transparency**: Share progress AND problems early
